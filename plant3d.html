About

Machines are laid out in flowsheet order along the hall. A node fed from a secondary port, or from a port another node already uses, gets its own belt lane. Unused ports end in bins. Fragments shrink at crushers and shredders and are routed at separators with the same partition curves the sim uses, so a slider change moves the traffic at once. Bin fill follows the sim's steady-state bin masses as the batch progresses.
